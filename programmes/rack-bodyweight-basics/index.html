Rack Bodyweight Basics

A no-equipment entry plan for building consistency before adding external load.

Best for: users with no gym access or users starting from a very simple setup.

Schedule: short full-body sessions with rest days between harder efforts.

How it works

  • Sessions use push, pull, leg and core patterns that can be done without equipment.
  • Progression comes from reps, tempo, range of motion and harder variations.
  • The aim is consistency first, then a move into loaded training when possible.

Progression

Add reps until the target is comfortable, then move to a harder variation or slower tempo. Keep form strict before increasing difficulty.
