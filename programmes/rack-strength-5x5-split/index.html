Rack Strength 5x5 Split

A four-day upper/lower version of Rack Strength 5x5 for shorter, more focused sessions.

Best for: lifters who prefer four shorter sessions instead of three longer full-body sessions.

Schedule: two lower-body days and two upper-body days per week.

How it works

  • Lower days focus on squat, hinge and leg accessory work.
  • Upper days focus on presses, rows, pulls and upper-body accessories.
  • Training stress is spread through the week instead of packed into full-body days.

Progression

Main lifts still progress when the target sets and reps are completed. Because sessions are more frequent, keep rest days and sleep consistent.
