Rack Fitness Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 May 2026

Rack Fitness is a workout tracking and strength training app. We only use personal information to provide the app, sync your account, process subscriptions through Apple, support workout and body-progress features, and improve reliability.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Rack Fitness, the app may process:

  • Account information, such as your sign-in identifier when you choose to create or use an account.
  • Workout information, such as exercises, sets, reps, weights, workout history, notes, and progress records you enter in the app.
  • Body and measurement information, such as weight or other measurements you choose to enter or permit the app to read.
  • Purchase information handled by Apple, such as subscription status and entitlement information needed to unlock Pro features.
  • Technical information, such as device, app version, diagnostics, and basic usage signals needed to operate and improve the app.

Health Data

Rack Fitness may integrate with Apple Health if you grant permission. With your permission, the app may read supported body measurement data and write workout data to Apple Health. Health data is used only to provide the app features you choose to use. Rack Fitness does not use Apple Health data for advertising or sell Apple Health data.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide workout tracking, progress, and subscription features.
  • Sync account-backed app data where applicable.
  • Restore purchases and manage access to paid features.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain app security, reliability, and performance.

Third-Party Services

Rack Fitness may use service providers to operate the app, including Apple services for subscriptions and HealthKit permissions, authentication and backend infrastructure, and hosting or database providers used to store account-backed app data. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services to the app.

Subscriptions and Payments

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Rack Fitness does not receive or store your full payment card details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where necessary to operate Rack Fitness, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a business transfer if the app is ever sold or reorganised.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Rack Fitness, maintain records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and support account recovery or deletion requests.

Your Choices

You can control Apple Health permissions in the iOS Health app and device settings. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings. You can contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion of account-backed information.

Children

Rack Fitness is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy as Rack Fitness changes. If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@getrackfitness.com.
