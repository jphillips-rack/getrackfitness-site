Rack Fitness Support

Need help with Rack Fitness? Contact support and include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue.

Contact

Email support@getrackfitness.com.

Subscriptions

Rack Fitness subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in the App Store app or in iOS Settings under your Apple ID subscriptions.

Account and Data Requests

For account help, data access, correction, or deletion requests, email support@getrackfitness.com.
