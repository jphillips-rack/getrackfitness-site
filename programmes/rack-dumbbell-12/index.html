Rack Dumbbell

A dumbbell-only plan for home gyms, hotel gyms, travel blocks or periods without a barbell setup.

Best for: users training with dumbbells and bodyweight only.

Schedule: full-body sessions three times per week.

How it works

  • Sessions use dumbbell squat, hinge, press, row and core patterns.
  • Progression uses reps, load and harder variations when dumbbell jumps are limited.
  • The plan keeps training consistent until fuller equipment is available.

Progression

When all reps are completed, add load if possible. If dumbbells jump too much, add reps or slow the tempo before increasing weight.
