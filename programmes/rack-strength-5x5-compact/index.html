Rack Strength 5x5 Compact

A short-session maintenance plan for keeping strength training moving when time is limited.

Best for: travel, busy weeks, or users who need simple short workouts.

Schedule: two compact full-body workouts per week.

How it works

  • Only the highest-priority lifts are included.
  • Sessions stay short by limiting exercise count and volume.
  • The goal is momentum and maintenance, not maximum weekly volume.

Progression

Use conservative jumps. If the short plan is being used during a stressful period, repeating the same load is still productive.
