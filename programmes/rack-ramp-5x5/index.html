Rack Ramp 5x5

A graduation option for lifters who are past beginner progression and need heavier, more structured loading.

Best for: intermediate users who no longer progress well by adding weight every session.

Schedule: three full-body workouts per week using heavy, light and medium structure.

How it works

  • Loading ramps toward heavier top sets rather than using identical sets across the session.
  • Weekly progression is slower than the beginner 5x5 approach.
  • Back-off and lighter work helps manage fatigue while preserving practice.

Progression

Progress in smaller weekly steps. If top sets are missed, repeat the week or reduce load before building again.
