Rack Strength 5x5 Build

A three-day plan for lifters who want the 5x5 strength base with more muscle-building accessory work.

Best for: users choosing Build Muscle who still want a clear barbell strength structure.

Schedule: three sessions per week with recovery days between hard sessions.

How it works

  • Two full-body strength days keep the main lifts progressing.
  • The upper-focused day adds more chest, back, shoulders, arms and core work.
  • Main lifts use lower reps; accessory lifts use moderate hypertrophy reps.

Progression

Increase main lift weights after completed work sets. Keep accessory work controlled and progress with reps first, then load when the target is consistently met.

Use this instead of standard 5x5 when

You can recover from slightly more volume and want visible upper-body work without losing the simplicity of a set programme.
