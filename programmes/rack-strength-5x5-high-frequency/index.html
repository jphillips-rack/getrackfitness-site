Rack Strength 5x5 High Frequency

A five-day upper/lower plan with extra upper-body exposure for users who can recover from more frequent lifting.

Best for: intermediate users with reliable recovery, nutrition and schedule.

Schedule: five sessions per week: two lower days and three upper days.

How it works

  • Upper-body lifts appear more often than in the standard plan.
  • Lower-body training is still present but managed to avoid burying recovery.
  • Accessory work supports pressing, rowing and shoulder stability.

Progression

Progress successful lifts gradually. If performance drops across multiple sessions, repeat the load or reduce volume before pushing heavier.
